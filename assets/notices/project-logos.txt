Project starter logos
====================

Original project artwork is used to identify the corresponding starters.
The artwork, project names and trademarks remain with their respective owners.
The accompanying project-logo-*-LICENSE files preserve available upstream notices.
SVG XML declarations and DOCTYPE headers were removed for embedding; Lime's editor namespace entities were expanded. Shapes and colours are unchanged.
PNG assets are unmodified. Raster logos are decoded only for mounted cards, reduced for display and disposed with them.

# Image previews

PNG, JPEG (`.jpg`, `.jpe`, `.jpeg`), BMP, GIF, ICO, WebP, AVIF and SVG files open in an image tab. GIF previews show the first frame.

Drag to pan and scroll to zoom. Toolbar actions fit the image, show it at 100%, change background contrast or refresh the preview.

## SVG source

Use the toolbar to switch between preview and source. The preview includes unsaved edits, and switching preserves undo history. Explorer also offers **Open Preview** and **Open in Editor** for SVG files.

For images embedded in documents, see [Markdown preview](markdown.md#html-and-images).
